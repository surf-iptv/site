Privacy Policy

Effective from 24 November 2026. Last change: the free trial is now 7 days, and what is kept on your device when it ends.

This policy covers Surf IPTV (app ID com.surfiptv), a media player for phones and Google TV / Android TV. In this policy, “we” means the developer of Surf IPTV. Contact: contact@surf-iptv.com.

In short

Surf IPTV is a media player. It does not create an account with us and does not send us any data. There are no ads, no analytics and nothing that tracks what you watch.

All connections to servers are made directly from your device to the addresses you add; the app also sends the User-Agent and Referer headers set in your playlist or in the app's settings. Whether a connection is encrypted (HTTPS) depends on the address you enter.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone. It reads or writes a file only when you pick a playlist file, or save or restore a backup yourself, in the place you choose. Fonts are built into the app, so it does not connect to font servers.

App data is not included in your Google account backup (allowBackup=false).

Content

Surf IPTV does not contain, host, distribute, select, sell or recommend any channels, movies, series, playlists or stream addresses, and it is not affiliated with any TV channel, content owner or IPTV provider. Everything you watch comes from the sources you add yourself. You are responsible for having the right to use them.

Purchases (Premium)

Surf IPTV is free to download. For 7 days after you install it, all Premium features work for free. The app counts this trial itself, on your device: it does not start a subscription, needs no payment details and ends by itself, and the free features keep working afterwards. When the trial ends, your own channel setup (order, names, hidden channels and categories) is kept on the device and comes back if you buy Premium. Premium is an optional in-app purchase: a yearly subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase. You manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play. The payment is processed by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive your name, email address or card details. The app uses the Google Play Billing Library only to show the price, start the purchase and check on your device whether Premium is active; it keeps just that status (and whether it comes from the yearly plan or Lifetime) and the trial start date on the device.

The Google Play Billing Library is a Google component built into the app. Besides talking to the Google Play app on your device, it sends Google its own technical logs about the billing service, for example whether connecting to Google Play or loading the prices worked. These logs are processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; they never contain your playlists, login details or what you watch, and we do not receive them. The app itself contains no analytics or advertising libraries.

If you contact us

If you write to us or send us a report with the "Share" button (for example by email to contact@surf-iptv.com), we receive only what you send: your email address, your message and the report. A report may include your server address and username, but never your password. We use it only to answer you and fix the problem, we do not share it with anyone, and we delete it when it is no longer needed for that. You can ask us at any time to delete it sooner.

Deleting your data

  • Some data: removing a playlist (Settings → Your playlist → Remove playlist) deletes its login details, favorites and downloaded lists; Clear list cache removes the downloaded lists, and Clear in the playback log empties the log.
  • All data: uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Surf IPTV → Storage → Clear data.

The app does not send us any data, so there is nothing to delete on our side – except messages and reports you send us yourself (see above).

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes

We will announce changes to this policy in the update notes on Google Play. The current version is always at this address and in the app under Settings → Privacy policy.
